# StudyMate AI: user guide

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** with your email and a password. You can reset the password from the login page.
2. Answer the three short questions (your name, subjects, and a daily XP goal). You can change them later in Settings.
3. **Add notes** on the dashboard in any of these ways: drop a PDF, Word file, EPUB, text file or photo; take a photo of handwritten notes; paste text; paste a web address or a YouTube link; or upload a lecture recording.

## Studying one set of notes

Open it from the Library. The tabs along the top are:

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Choose **Entire lesson** or **Topic by topic**, and **Beginner**, **Intermediate** or **Advanced**. Beginner is only the key points; Advanced is exam depth. Press **Read aloud**, or play the **audio overview**, a two-host conversation about the topic. Select text to **Highlight** it or add a note. |
| **Notes** | Your original text, one topic at a time. |
| **Flashcards** | Flip a card, then rate it Again, Hard, Good or Easy. Cards you find hard come back sooner. Add, edit and delete your own, and export to Anki, a spreadsheet or PDF. |
| **Quizzes** | Make a quiz for the whole lesson or one topic. Pick question types (multiple choice, true or false, fill in the blank, short written answer). Turn on **Mock exam** for a timed test with no hints. |
| **Explain** | Teach a topic back in your own words, typed or spoken. The AI marks it against your notes: what you got right and what to add. |
| **Mind map** | The lesson on one page. |
| **Cheat sheet** | A dense one-page revision sheet you can print. |
| **Highlights** | Everything you highlighted, with your notes. |

The **share** button on the top right makes a link a friend can open and copy into their own library. You can turn the link off any time.

## Quizzes and weak topics

After a quiz you see a score for every topic. A topic under 70% is marked **Needs review**, with three buttons: **Summarize it for me**, **Read it myself**, and **Try this topic again**. **Mixed quiz** (on the Quizzes page) combines several sets of notes, which is handy before a subject exam.

## Planning

On **Planner**, add an exam with its date and the notes it covers. Each day you get a short list: flashcards due, weak topics first, then topics you have not read. In the last three days it adds a timed mock exam.

## Staying motivated

- **XP, levels and streaks:** every study action earns XP. A **streak freeze** covers one missed day.
- **Daily challenges:** three a day; finish one and claim bonus XP.
- **Badges** are on the Achievements page.
- **Focus timer:** 15, 25 or 45 minutes; you earn 1 XP per finished minute.
- **Study groups:** make a group, share its six-letter code, and compare weekly XP. Members see only your display name and weekly XP.
- **The study cat** in the corner follows your cursor, likes being petted, and gives study tips when clicked. Hide it with the small x. It stays out of the way during quizzes.

## Language, comfort and privacy

- **Language button** (top bar): English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada or Malayalam. Summaries, quizzes, flashcards, chat and audio are written in that language.
- **Settings > Reading comfort:** larger text, an easier-to-read font, more spacing, fewer animations.
- **Settings > Reminders:** a daily nudge that works while the app is open or installed.
- **Install it** as an app from your browser's menu. The shell then opens even without a connection.
- **Settings > Your data:** download everything as a file, or delete your account and all your notes.

## Ask the Sidekick

The chat button (bottom right) answers questions. With a document open it answers from that document; otherwise it searches across all your notes and tells you which one it used.

## Good to know

- The AI can make mistakes. Check anything important against your original notes.
- If you see "You are going a little fast", wait the number of seconds shown.
- Some websites do not allow automated reading. Save the page as a PDF and upload that instead.
